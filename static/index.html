route Simple 3-Step Process

From invoice to filed.

Your entire GST workflow, automated.

1

Client sends invoice on WhatsApp

Your client forwards any invoice — a camera photo, a downloaded PDF, or a scanned document — to the GST Autopilot WhatsApp number. Our bot immediately acknowledges receipt.

2

AI extracts, validates, and stores data

Gemini Vision AI analyzes the invoice, extracts all fields, validates GSTIN format, checks ITC eligibility, and verifies the math. A structured summary is sent back to the client instantly.

In the CA Dashboard, you review each invoice side-by-side with the original image, make corrections if needed, approve records, and export a ready-to-upload GSTR-1 or GSTR-3B JSON file.
