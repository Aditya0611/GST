# Place of Supply Rules Under GST

## Why Does Place of Supply Matter?

The Place of Supply (PoS) determines whether a transaction is:
*   **Intra-state** → CGST + SGST applies
*   **Inter-state** → IGST applies

The correct PoS is critical for charging the right type of tax and for the buyer to claim ITC correctly.

## Place of Supply — Goods (Section 10, IGST Act)

### Movement of Goods (Most Common)
*   PoS = **place where movement of goods terminates** (i.e., the delivery location).
*   Example: Goods dispatched from Mumbai to Delhi → PoS = Delhi → IGST applicable.

### Goods Installed or Assembled
*   PoS = **place of installation or assembly**.

### Goods on Board a Conveyance (Ship, Aircraft, Train, Bus)
*   PoS = **place where goods are taken on board**.

### No Movement of Goods
*   PoS = **location of goods at the time of delivery**.

## Place of Supply — Services (Section 12, IGST Act — B2C)

| Type of Service | Place of Supply |
|---|---|
| Services to a registered person (B2B) | Location of the recipient (GSTIN state) |
| Services related to immovable property (construction, architect, interior design) | Location of the immovable property |
| Restaurant, catering, accommodation | Location where the service is actually performed |
| Training and performance appraisal (B2C) | Location where the service is performed |
| Admission to events (concerts, sports) | Place where the event is held |
| Transportation of goods (other than mail/courier) | Location of the recipient (if registered); location where goods are handed over (if unregistered) |
| Passenger transport | Place where the passenger embarks |
| Telecom and internet services | Location of billing address of subscriber |
| Banking and financial services | Location of recipient (or location of service provider if recipient location unknown) |
| Insurance services | Location of recipient |
| Online information and database access (OIDAR) | Location of recipient |

## Place of Supply — B2B Services (Section 12(2))

When services are provided to a **GST-registered recipient**:
*   PoS = **Location of the recipient** (as per GSTIN).
*   This is the default rule for B2B service transactions.
*   Example: A Mumbai company provides consulting to a Delhi-registered company → PoS = Delhi → IGST.

## Place of Supply — Exports and Imports

*   **Exports of goods:** PoS = outside India → **zero-rated** (no GST or IGST with refund option).
*   **Exports of services:** PoS = outside India if payment received in foreign currency AND recipient is outside India → **zero-rated**.
*   **Imports of goods:** PoS = location of importer → **IGST** applies.
*   **Imports of services:** PoS = location of recipient in India → **IGST under RCM**.

## Common Mistakes in Place of Supply

1.  **Charging CGST+SGST on inter-state supply:** If goods are delivered to another state, IGST must be charged, not CGST+SGST. The buyer cannot claim CGST/SGST paid as ITC against IGST liability.
2.  **Wrong state code on invoice:** Leads to wrong PoS determination and mismatch in GSTR-2B.
3.  **Construction services:** PoS is always where the property is located, not where the contractor's office is.
4.  **Software/IT services (B2C):** PoS = location of recipient, not location of IT company.

## Place of Supply — Special Cases

### Works Contract Services (Construction)
*   PoS = **Location of immovable property**.
*   Even if both supplier and recipient are in the same state, if the property is in another state, IGST applies.

### Goods Sent for Exhibition or On Approval
*   PoS = **Principal place of business of the sender** if goods are sent to another state for up to 6 months.

### Supply on High Sea / Bill to Ship to
*   **Bill-to Ship-to (Triangle Supply):** PoS = location stated in the invoice (location of actual delivery).

## How to Determine GSTIN State from GSTIN

*   First **2 digits** of a 15-character GSTIN = **State code**.
*   Example: GSTIN `27AABC1234M1Z5` → State code 27 = Maharashtra.

| State Code | State |
|---|---|
| 01 | Jammu & Kashmir |
| 06 | Haryana |
| 07 | Delhi |
| 08 | Rajasthan |
| 09 | Uttar Pradesh |
| 19 | West Bengal |
| 27 | Maharashtra |
| 29 | Karnataka |
| 32 | Kerala |
| 33 | Tamil Nadu |
| 36 | Telangana |
