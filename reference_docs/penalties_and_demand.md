# GST Penalties, Offences, and Demand & Recovery

## Common GST Offences and Penalties

### Section 122 — Penalty for Specific Offences
Any registered person who commits any of the following offences is liable to a penalty of **₹10,000 OR the tax evaded/short paid (whichever is higher)**:

| Offence | Example |
|---|---|
| Supplying goods/services without issuing invoice | Not giving a bill to customer |
| Issuing invoice without actual supply | Fake invoices (bogus billing) |
| Collecting GST but not paying to government within 3 months | Collecting tax from buyers but pocketing it |
| Taking ITC without actual receipt of goods/services | Fake ITC claims on paper |
| Distributing ITC in excess of ITC available | ISD over-distribution |
| Falsifying/substituting financial records | Manipulating accounts |
| Failing to maintain books of account | Not keeping records |
| Failing to register for GST when required | Avoiding mandatory registration |
| Furnishing false information in returns | Wrong figures in GSTR-3B |
| Obstructing or preventing a GST officer | Obstruction during GST audit |

### Section 125 — General Penalty
For any contravention not specifically covered: penalty up to **₹25,000**.

| Return | Late Fee (with tax liability) | Late Fee (nil return) |
|---|---|---|
| GSTR-1 | ₹50/day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST), max ₹10,000 | ₹20/day, max ₹500 |
| GSTR-3B | ₹50/day, max ₹10,000 | ₹20/day, max ₹500 |
| GSTR-9 (Annual) | ₹200/day (₹100 CGST + ₹100 SGST) | Max: 0.25% of turnover in the state |
| GSTR-4 (Composition) | ₹50/day, max ₹2,000 | ₹20/day, max ₹500 |

## Interest on Late Payment of Tax

*   **Interest @ 18% per annum** on tax paid after due date (from due date to date of payment).
*   **Interest @ 24% per annum** in cases of undue or excess claim of ITC or undue or excess reduction of output tax liability.
*   Interest is calculated on the **net tax liability** (after ITC set-off) for delayed GSTR-3B payments.

## Demand and Recovery — Sections 73 & 74

### Section 73 — Tax Not Paid/Short Paid (No Fraud)
Applicable when tax has not been paid or short paid due to **genuine mistake, ignorance, or inadvertence** (not involving fraud or wilful misstatement).

*   **Penalty:** 10% of tax due (minimum ₹10,000).
*   If paid within 30 days of show-cause notice: **No penalty** (only tax + interest).
*   Time limit for issuing demand: **3 years** from due date of annual return.

### Section 74 — Tax Not Paid Due to Fraud
Applicable when tax has not been paid due to **fraud, wilful misstatement, or suppression of facts**.

*   **Penalty:** 100% of tax due (equal to the tax amount).
*   If paid before issuance of show-cause notice: penalty = **15% of tax**.
*   If paid within 30 days of show-cause notice: penalty = **25% of tax**.
*   If paid within 30 days of order: penalty = **50% of tax**.
*   Time limit for issuing demand: **5 years** from due date of annual return.

---

## Tax Evasion — Arrest Provisions (Section 132)

Persons can be **arrested** for the following offences if tax evaded exceeds ₹2 crore:
*   Issuing invoices without actual supply.
*   Collecting GST but not depositing to government.
*   Taking/distributing ITC fraudulently.
*   Obtaining fraudulent refunds.

For tax evasion above ₹5 crore: offence is **non-bailable** and punishable with imprisonment up to **5 years**.

## GST Audit

### Department Audit (Section 65)
*   GST Commissioner can order audit of any registered person.
*   Audit must be completed within **3 months** (extendable to 6 months).
*   Taxpayer must provide records and returns within **15 days** of notice.

### Special Audit (Section 66)
*   If complexity requires, a Chartered Accountant or Cost Accountant can be directed to audit the books.

### Annual Audit / GSTR-9C (Section 35(5))
*   Taxpayers with turnover **> ₹5 crore** must get their books audited by a CA/CMA and file GSTR-9C (reconciliation statement).

| GSTR-9C Reconciliation | 31st December of following FY |
