# GST Composition Scheme Rules

## What is the Composition Scheme?

The Composition Scheme is a simplified GST compliance option for **small taxpayers**. Instead of filing detailed returns and paying tax on each transaction, composition dealers pay a **fixed percentage of their turnover** as tax and file **quarterly returns**.

## Eligibility — Who Can Opt?

### Turnover Threshold
*   Registered taxpayers with **aggregate annual turnover up to ₹1.5 crore** in the preceding financial year can opt for the composition scheme.
*   For **North-Eastern states and Uttarakhand:** Threshold is **₹75 lakh**.
*   **Service providers (Section 10(2A)):** Small service providers with turnover up to **₹50 lakh** can opt for a composition-like scheme.

### Who CANNOT Opt for Composition Scheme?
*   Persons making **inter-state outward supplies** of goods.
*   Persons supplying **non-taxable goods** under GST (e.g., alcohol for human consumption).
*   Persons supplying goods through an **e-commerce operator** who collects TCS (e.g., selling on Amazon, Flipkart).
*   **Casual taxable persons** and **non-resident taxable persons**.
*   Manufacturers of notified goods (e.g., ice cream, pan masala, tobacco).
*   Persons registered under the **Input Service Distributor** provisions.

## Tax Rates Under Composition Scheme

| Category | Tax Rate (on Turnover) |
|---|---|
| Manufacturers (other than notified goods) | 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST) |
| Restaurants not serving alcohol | 5% (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST) |
| Traders (buying and selling goods) | 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST) |
| Mixed supply (goods + services, services ≤ 10% of turnover) | 1% |
| Service providers under Section 10(2A) | 6% (3% CGST + 3% SGST) |

*   Tax is calculated on the **total turnover** (not profit or value-added).
*   IGST is **not applicable** — composition dealers can only make intra-state supplies.

## Key Restrictions for Composition Dealers

1.  **Cannot issue Tax Invoices** — must issue a **Bill of Supply** instead.
2.  **Cannot collect GST from customers** — the tax burden is on the dealer, not passed on.
3.  **Cannot claim Input Tax Credit (ITC)** — ITC on purchases is not available.
4.  **Cannot make inter-state sales** of goods (can receive inter-state purchases).
5.  Must display "Composition Taxable Person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies" on every bill of supply and at the place of business.

## Compliance — Filing Requirements

| Return | Frequency | Due Date | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| CMP-08 (Self-assessed tax payment) | Quarterly | 18th of month after quarter-end | Payment of tax for the quarter |
| GSTR-4 (Annual return) | Annual | 30th April of following FY | Summary of all supplies and tax paid |

*   No monthly returns required (unlike regular taxpayers).
*   No need to maintain invoice-level records for GSTR-1.

## Opting In and Out

*   **Opting In:** File Form GST CMP-02 at the beginning of the financial year (1st April). New registrations can opt in at the time of registration.
*   **Opting Out:** File Form GST CMP-04 when turnover exceeds the threshold or when the dealer wishes to switch back. They must then file regular returns from the next quarter.
*   Once opted out, cannot re-opt for the **same financial year**.

## Supplies Under RCM for Composition Dealers

*   Composition dealers are still liable to pay **GST under Reverse Charge Mechanism** on notified purchases.
*   This RCM payment is made in **cash** and is **not included** in the composition tax rate calculation.

## Important Scenario — ITC on Goods in Stock When Opting In

*   When a regular taxpayer opts for the composition scheme, they must **reverse the ITC** availed on:
    *   Inputs in stock
    *   Semi-finished / finished goods in stock
    *   Capital goods (proportional reversal)
*   This reversal must be done in Form GST ITC-03.

## Composition Scheme vs. Regular Scheme — Quick Comparison

| Feature | Composition Scheme | Regular Scheme |
|---|---|---|
| Turnover limit | Up to ₹1.5 crore | No limit |
| Tax rate | Flat % on turnover | As per HSN rate |
| ITC | Not available | Available |
| Inter-state sales | Not allowed (goods) | Allowed |
| Returns | Quarterly (CMP-08) + Annual (GSTR-4) | Monthly GSTR-1 + GSTR-3B |
| Invoice type | Bill of Supply | Tax Invoice |
| Tax collection from buyer | Not allowed | Mandatory |
