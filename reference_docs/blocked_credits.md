# Blocked Credits — Section 17(5) of the CGST Act

Even if goods or services are used in the course or furtherance of business, Input Tax Credit (ITC) is strictly blocked / ineligible for specific items under Section 17(5) of the CGST Act.

## 1. Blocked Items (No ITC Allowed)
The following purchases are categorized as blocked credits:
*   **Food and Beverages:** Outdoor catering, food, beverages, and restaurants. Exception: If it is used to make an outward taxable supply of the same category (e.g., a catering company buying food ingredients).
*   **Motor Vehicles & Conveyances:** Motor vehicles for passenger transport with approved seating capacity of 13 or fewer people (including the driver). Exception: If they are used for making further taxable supplies (like passenger transportation business or driving school).
*   **Club, Health, and Fitness Memberships:** Memberships for clubs, gyms, wellness, and fitness centers.
*   **Travel Benefits to Employees:** Travel benefits extended to employees on vacation such as leave travel concession (LTC) or home travel concession. Exception: If it is statutory duty/obligatory for the employer to provide it under law.
*   **Works Contract Services:** Works contract services for construction of an immovable property (other than plant and machinery). Exception: If it is an input service for further works contract supply.
*   **Goods Lost, Stolen, or Destroyed:** Goods written off, gifted, stolen, destroyed, or distributed as free samples.
*   **Personal Consumption:** Any goods or services used for personal consumption rather than business operations.

## 2. General Exceptions (Obligatory under Law)
Input tax credit in respect of food and beverages, outdoor catering, beauty treatment, health services, cosmetic and plastic surgery, life insurance, and health insurance is **allowed** if it is obligatory for an employer to provide the same to its employees under any law for the time being in force (e.g. mandatory health insurance for workers during a pandemic or specific factory laws).
