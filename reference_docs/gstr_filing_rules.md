# GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B Filing Rules

## What is GSTR-1?

GSTR-1 is a monthly or quarterly return that every registered GST taxpayer (except composition dealers, input service distributors, non-resident taxpayers) must file. It contains details of all **outward supplies (sales)** made during the period.

## GSTR-1 Due Dates

*   **Monthly filers (turnover > ₹5 crore in previous FY):** 11th of the following month.
    *   Example: GSTR-1 for July must be filed by **11th August**.
*   **Quarterly filers (turnover ≤ ₹5 crore, QRMP scheme):** 13th of the month following the quarter-end.
    *   Example: GSTR-1 for April–June quarter must be filed by **13th July**.
*   **Invoice Furnishing Facility (IFF):** QRMP dealers can upload B2B invoices monthly (by 13th of next month) without filing full GSTR-1.

## What to Report in GSTR-1?

| Table | Details |
|---|---|
| B2B Supplies | Invoices issued to GST-registered buyers (with GSTIN) |
| B2C Large (B2CL) | Invoices > ₹2.5 lakh issued to unregistered buyers for inter-state supply |
| B2C Small (B2CS) | Summary of other B2C supplies (intra-state and inter-state ≤ ₹2.5 lakh) |
| Exports (EXPWP/EXPWOP) | Supplies exported with or without GST payment |
| Credit/Debit Notes | CDN and CDNUR details |
| Advances | Advance receipts and adjustments |
| HSN Summary | HSN-wise summary of outward supplies |

## What is GSTR-3B?

GSTR-3B is a **monthly self-declaration summary return** showing:
*   Total outward taxable supplies and output tax
*   Eligible Input Tax Credit (ITC) claimed
*   Tax paid (IGST, CGST, SGST/UTGST, Cess)
*   Net tax liability after ITC set-off

## GSTR-3B Due Dates

*   **Turnover > ₹5 crore:** **20th of the following month** (e.g., July's GSTR-3B by 20th August).
*   **Turnover ≤ ₹5 crore (Category A states):** **22nd of the following month**.
*   **Turnover ≤ ₹5 crore (Category B states):** **24th of the following month**.

## Key Rules for GSTR-3B

*   ITC claimed in GSTR-3B should not exceed 110% of the ITC available in GSTR-2B (auto-populated from supplier's GSTR-1).
*   GSTR-3B must be filed **even if there is no business activity** (nil return).
*   Filing GSTR-3B is mandatory before filing GSTR-1 for the next period.
*   Late filing attracts interest @ **18% per annum** on outstanding tax, plus a **late fee** of ₹50/day (₹20/day for nil returns), capped at ₹10,000.

## Relationship Between GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B

*   GSTR-1 feeds into buyer's **GSTR-2B** (auto-populated ITC statement).
*   Suppliers must file GSTR-1 on time so that buyers can claim ITC through GSTR-2B.
*   If a supplier does not file GSTR-1, the buyer's ITC may not appear in GSTR-2B.

## GSTR-2B

*   Auto-generated monthly ITC statement based on GSTR-1, GSTR-5, and GSTR-6 filed by suppliers.
*   Available on the **14th of the following month** (for monthly filers).
*   Taxpayers should reconcile GSTR-2B with their purchase register before filing GSTR-3B.

## Annual Return — GSTR-9

*   Filed annually by all registered taxpayers (except composition dealers, casual taxpayers, etc.).
*   Due date: **31st December** of the following financial year.
*   GSTR-9C (reconciliation statement + audit certificate): mandatory for taxpayers with turnover > ₹5 crore.
