# Input Tax Credit (ITC) Eligibility — Section 16 of the CGST Act

To claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) under Indian GST law, a business must satisfy all of the statutory conditions outlined in Section 16 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act.

## 1. Primary Eligibility Conditions
A registered taxable person can only claim ITC on goods or services if they are used or intended to be used in the course or furtherance of their business.

## 2. Core Conditions for Claiming Credit
No registered person shall be entitled to claim any Input Tax Credit unless all of the following conditions are met:
*   **Possession of a Tax Invoice:** The buyer must possess a valid tax invoice, debit note, or bill of entry issued by the registered supplier.
*   **Actual Receipt of Goods or Services:** The buyer must have actually received the goods or services. Deemed receipt rules apply if goods are delivered to a third party on the direction of the buyer (Bill-to-Ship-to model).
*   **Tax Paid to the Government:** The supplier must have paid the tax charged on the supply to the government, either in cash or by utilizing eligible input tax credit.
*   **Filing of Valid Returns:** The buyer must have filed their regular monthly or quarterly GST return (GSTR-3B).
*   **Reconciliation (Section 16(2)(aa)):** The supplier must report the invoice in their GSTR-1, and it must appear in the buyer's auto-generated GSTR-2B statement.

## 3. Payment to Supplier Within 180 Days
*   The buyer must pay the supplier the value of the supply along with the tax within 180 days from the date of the invoice.
*   If the buyer fails to pay within 180 days, the ITC claimed must be reversed, and the buyer is liable to pay interest on the reversed amount. Once the payment is subsequently made, the buyer can re-claim the ITC.

## 4. Depreciation Double-Dip Restriction
*   If a business capitalized the tax component of a capital good and claimed depreciation on the tax amount under the Income Tax Act, they cannot claim Input Tax Credit under GST for that tax component.
