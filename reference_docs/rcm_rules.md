# Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) Under GST

## What is Reverse Charge Mechanism?

Under normal GST, the **supplier** collects and pays tax to the government. Under the **Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)**, the **recipient (buyer)** is liable to pay the GST directly to the government instead of the supplier.

## Two Types of RCM

### 1. Section 9(3) — Notified Goods and Services
The government has specifically notified certain goods and services where RCM always applies, regardless of whether the supplier is registered or not.

**Common notified services under RCM:**

| Service | Supplier | Recipient |
|---|---|---|
| Legal services by an advocate | Individual advocate / firm | Business entity |
| Services by a director to the company | Director (in individual capacity) | Company / LLP |
| Insurance agent services | Insurance agent | Insurance company |
| Recovery agent services | Recovery agent | Banking company / NBFC |
| Transport of goods by GTA (Goods Transport Agency) | GTA | Registered person, factory, body corporate, society, co-op society, partnership, casual taxable person |
| Passenger transport by radio-taxi, motor cab, maxicab | E-commerce operator | Passenger |
| Import of services | Foreign supplier | Indian recipient |
| Renting or leasing of immovable property by government to business | Government, local authority | Registered business |
| Services supplied by an arbitral tribunal | Arbitral tribunal | Business entity |

**Common notified goods under RCM:**

| Goods | Supplier |
|---|---|
| Cashew nuts (not shelled or peeled) | Agricultural labourer / agriculturist |
| Bidi wrapper leaves (tendu) | Agricultural labourer / agriculturist |
| Tobacco leaves | Agricultural labourer / agriculturist |
| Lottery | State government / lottery distributor |
| Silk yarn | Silk yarn manufacturer |
| Raw cotton | Agricultural labourer / agriculturist |
| Priority sector lending certificate | Any registered / unregistered person |

### 2. Section 9(4) — Purchases from Unregistered Suppliers
*   A registered person purchasing **specified goods/services** from an **unregistered supplier** may attract RCM.
*   Currently, Section 9(4) is applicable only to specific notified sectors (e.g., real estate promoters buying from unregistered persons).
*   For most regular businesses, Section 9(4) RCM has been deferred/suspended.

## Key Rules for RCM

*   **Registration:** Even if turnover is below the threshold, a person liable to pay tax under RCM **must register** for GST.
*   **Self-Invoice:** When purchasing from an unregistered supplier under RCM, the recipient must issue a **self-invoice**.
*   **Payment of Tax:** RCM tax must be paid in **cash** (cannot be paid using ITC balance).
*   **ITC on RCM:** ITC of the GST paid under RCM can be claimed by the recipient in the **same return period** (subject to other ITC eligibility conditions).
*   **Time of Supply under RCM:** Earlier of:
    *   Date of payment
    *   Date immediately after 60 days from the date of invoice

## GTA (Goods Transport Agency) — Common RCM Scenario

*   If a registered business hires a GTA for transport of goods, **RCM @ 5% GST** applies (no ITC to GTA).
*   Alternatively, GTA can opt to pay GST @ 12% (forward charge), in which case the business can claim ITC.
*   GTA must declare their option at the beginning of each financial year.
*   **No RCM** if the consignment value is ≤ ₹750 for a single consignee.
*   **No RCM** if the consignment is transported in a non-motorised vehicle.

## Import of Services — Always RCM

*   Any import of services from abroad (even if the foreign supplier is not registered in India) attracts **IGST under RCM** on the Indian recipient.
*   This applies to subscription services, software licenses, consulting fees, etc. paid to foreign entities.

## RCM and Compliance

*   RCM liability must be reported in **Table 3.1(d)** of GSTR-3B.
*   ITC claimed on RCM must be reported in **Table 4(A)(3)** of GSTR-3B.
*   Failure to pay RCM attracts **interest @ 18% per annum** plus penalty.
